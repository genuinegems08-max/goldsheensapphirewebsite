Privacy

Privacy policy.

We are a small gemstone business, not an advertising company. We collect only what you send us in an inquiry, and we use it only to reply to you.

Last updated 22 July 2026

Who we are

This site is operated by Gold Sheen Sapphire (Genuine Gems & Jewellery), a dealer in gold sheen sapphire and other precious and semi-precious stones. You can reach us at genuinegems08@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you contact us. If you submit the inquiry form or email us, we receive what you choose to put in it — typically your name, email address and your message. If you contact us on WhatsApp or Instagram, we receive whatever those services show us about your account. We ask you not to send sensitive personal information; we have no need for it.

When you simply browse. This site runs no analytics, no advertising pixels and no tracking cookies. We do not build a profile of you and we cannot identify you from a visit alone. Our host and the services below do keep standard technical records, described next.

Third parties that process data for us

  • Web3Forms — delivers our contact form to our inbox. Your submission passes through their servers.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this site and keeps standard server logs, which include visitor IP addresses, for security and reliability.
  • Google Fonts — supplies the typefaces. Because the fonts load from Google's servers, your IP address is visible to Google when a page loads.

We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not share it with anyone else for marketing.

Why we use it, and for how long

We use your inquiry solely to answer it, to quote on stones, and to keep a record of a conversation we've had with you about an order. We keep inquiry emails for as long as the business relationship or a reasonable record of it is useful, and delete them on request.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it — email genuinegems08@gmail.com and we will act on it. Depending on where you live you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR; we will honour those requests regardless of where you are.

Our social media accounts

We publish our own product photography to our own business accounts on Instagram and Pinterest — both the Gold Sheen Sapphire accounts and our general accounts covering other precious and semi-precious stones. When you interact with us there, that platform's own privacy policy governs the data it collects about you — we only see what the platform chooses to show a business account. We do not import or store follower data.

Children

This site is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change how this site handles information — for example if we ever add analytics — we will update this page and the date above.
