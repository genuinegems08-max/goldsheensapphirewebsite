Trust & verification

Ten labs. Thirteen reports. The same conclusion.

Material from this deposit has been examined independently by ten recognised gemological laboratories across four countries. Every single report on file states the same thing: natural corundum, no indication of heating.

What's in a report

What these reports confirm, consistently.

Standards vary slightly by laboratory, but across all eleven reports on file, four things repeat without exception:

  • Natural corundum

    Every report identifies the material as natural sapphire — not synthetic, not a simulant.

  • No indication of heating

    Stated explicitly, in those or equivalent words, on every single report — from GIA to Lotus Gemology.

  • Recognised as "golden sheen"

    Multiple labs independently name the same trade term for the phenomenon, unprompted.

  • Kenyan origin, where tested

    Reports that include an origin opinion (AGL, ICA GemLab, Lotus Gemology) all point to Kenya.
